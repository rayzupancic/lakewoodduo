Who we are

A local owner, not a leasing office

Lakewood Duo LLC is a small, privately held property investment and management company working in the Denver metro area. We buy in neighborhoods we know, keep the properties in good repair, and manage them ourselves — which means the person who answers your message is the same person who handles the property.

Maintenance is handled promptly, updates are done properly rather than cheaply, and residents are treated like neighbors. That approach keeps our turnover low, which is why vacancies come up rarely.

  • Where we invest Lakewood and the surrounding west Denver metro
  • What we own Small residential — duplex units and single-family homes
  • How we manage Directly, by the owners, with local contractors
  • Current status All units occupied — inquiries welcome
